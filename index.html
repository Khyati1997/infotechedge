About InfotechEdge

A boutique IT consultancy built on real experience, Canadian values, and a commitment to delivering results.

Who we are

InfotechEdge is a Canadian IT consultancy serving businesses across the Greater Toronto Area and beyond. We were built on a simple belief: businesses deserve senior-level IT expertise without the overhead and bureaucracy of a large firm.

With over 10 years of hands-on experience across IT strategy, cybersecurity, cloud infrastructure, and contractor placement, we bring real-world knowledge to every engagement — not textbook advice.

We're bilingual, serving clients in both English and French, and we're committed to transparent pricing, honest advice, and long-term partnerships with every client we work with.
